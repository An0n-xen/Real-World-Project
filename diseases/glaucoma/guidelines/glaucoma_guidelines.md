# Clinical Guidelines for Glaucoma Diagnosis

## Overview
Glaucoma is a group of eye diseases that damage the optic nerve, often caused by
abnormally high pressure in the eye. It is one of the leading causes of irreversible
blindness worldwide.

## Key Diagnostic Indicators

### 1. Cup-to-Disc Ratio (CDR)
The cup-to-disc ratio is the primary quantitative indicator for glaucoma assessment.
- **Normal CDR**: 0.0 – 0.3
- **Suspicious CDR**: 0.3 – 0.6
- **Indicative of Glaucoma**: > 0.6
- A CDR asymmetry > 0.2 between eyes is also suspicious.

### 2. Neuroretinal Rim Assessment (ISNT Rule)
The neuroretinal rim should follow the ISNT rule:
- **I**nferior rim is the thickest
- **S**uperior rim is the second thickest
- **N**asal rim is the third thickest
- **T**emporal rim is the thinnest
Violation of the ISNT rule suggests glaucomatous damage.

### 3. Optic Disc Hemorrhages
- Flame-shaped hemorrhages at the disc margin
- Strongly associated with glaucoma progression
- Often found in the inferotemporal or superotemporal sectors

### 4. Peripapillary Atrophy (PPA)
- Beta-zone PPA (adjacent to disc margin) is associated with glaucoma
- Progressive enlargement of PPA suggests disease progression

### 5. Retinal Nerve Fiber Layer (RNFL) Defects
- Wedge-shaped RNFL defects visible on fundus photography
- Best observed with red-free photography
- Early indicator of glaucomatous damage

## Diagnostic Workflow

1. **Image Acquisition**: Obtain high-quality fundus photograph
2. **Optic Disc Segmentation**: Identify and segment the optic disc boundary
3. **Optic Cup Segmentation**: Identify and segment the optic cup boundary
4. **CDR Calculation**: Compute vertical cup-to-disc ratio
5. **Qualitative Assessment**: Evaluate ISNT rule, hemorrhages, PPA, and RNFL
6. **Clinical Decision**: Integrate all indicators for final diagnosis

## Grading Scale
- **Grade 0 (Normal)**: CDR < 0.3, intact ISNT, no hemorrhages
- **Grade 1 (Suspect)**: CDR 0.3-0.6, or mild ISNT violation
- **Grade 2 (Probable Glaucoma)**: CDR > 0.6, ISNT violation, or disc hemorrhage
- **Grade 3 (Definite Glaucoma)**: CDR > 0.7, severe rim loss, RNFL defects
